# Level 1, Task 1: EDA on Retail Sales Data

**Notebook:** `Retail_Sales_EDA.ipynb` | **Data:** `retail_sales_dataset.csv` (1,000 transactions, 2023; Kaggle "Retail Sales Dataset")

## What is covered
Data inspection, descriptive statistics, monthly and quarterly trends, customer age and gender, revenue by category and price point, a correlation heatmap, two extra insight charts, written observations after each chart, and business recommendations.

## Key findings
- The two premium price points (300 and 500) are 39.6% of transactions but **88.4% of revenue**.
- Average spend per transaction is highest for 18-25 year olds (500) and lowest for 56-65 (412); gender makes almost no difference.
- Revenue peaked in May and was weakest in September; Q4 was the best quarter.
- The dataset has only 3 categories and no product names, so "top 10 products" is adapted to categories and price points.

## Run it
Keep the CSV in the same folder as the notebook, then run all cells.
